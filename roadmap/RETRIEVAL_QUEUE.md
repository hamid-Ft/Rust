## Entry rules

- IDs use `Pnn-E`, `Pnn-D`, and `Pnn-T` for explain, debug, and transfer.
- `Due` uses a project/session checkpoint, not an arbitrary calendar reminder.
- Results are `recalled`, `partial`, or `missed`; link to evidence when practical.
- Never store a full worked solution in the prompt—the point is retrieval.
- Delete low-value syntax trivia and keep prompts about decisions, behavior, and risks.
